# Slips to write and photograph

28 new slips: **8 development** (D01-D08) and **20 held-out** (H01-H20). The two photos you already took count as the other two development slips. Everything is synthetic; the work orders and parts exist in the sample data.

## How to write them

One sheet of paper per slip, laid out like the earlier ones: title, `W/O #`, `Date`, `Taken by`, `Location`, then the columns **Part # | Description | Qty | Unit**, then `Signature`. Write the labels and draw the column lines, as you did before. Use a dark pen.

**Write exactly what each block says.** A blank is a blank: don't write `none` or a dash. If you make a real mistake on the paper, leave it, and correct the matching row in `labels.csv` to **what the paper actually says**.

**Styles** (this is one writer in four deliberate styles, and the evaluation will say so):

- **A**: your normal handwriting
- **B**: fast and slanted, the way you write when in a hurry
- **C**: ALL CAPITALS, small and tight
- **D**: your non-dominant hand (or a very different pen and grip)

**Photos:** flat on a table, whole page in frame, JPEG, saved as `data/eval/photos/<ID>.jpeg` (for example `D03.jpeg`), unless the block says dim light or an angle.

**Order:** write the 8 development slips first. Write the held-out slips in a separate sitting. Don't open the model's readings of the held-out set until we agree the rules are frozen.
